Accounts of the Imam's participation in battles (Badr, Uhud, Khaybar, Karbala, etc.), diplomatic events, and significant incidents. Extract each distinct event or episode as a record.

5. MIRACLE ACCOUNTS
Any account of a miracle or supernatural event attributed to an Imam.

Reports about an Imam's designation (naṣṣ) for the Imamate, circumstances of death, burial, children, and significant events of their Imamate.
